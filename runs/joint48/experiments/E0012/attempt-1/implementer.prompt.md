## The issue

### OR-of-ranges scan filter as dense-mask ExpressionFilter

**Hypothesis.** q19 (190ms) now has per-table OR filters, derived by E0001's JoinDependentFilter, on lineitem/part (for example OR of l_quantity BETWEEN ranges). These filters are evaluated as generic ExpressionFilters through the expression executor with a selection vector, which costs a lot per row. A prior run measured q19 -7..-11% from evaluating OR-of-range conjunctions as a dense mask over the full vector and then gathering into the selection.

**Plan.** In the ExpressionFilter scan path (src/planner/filter/expression_filter.cpp, and the place in ColumnSegment/ColumnData where expression filters are applied), detect when the expression is an OR of comparisons/BETWEENs on the filtered column against constants. When the incoming selection is sparse or full, evaluate the ranges branch-free over the dense vector into a bitmask, then build the output selection from the mask. Fall back to the existing path otherwise.

**Target files.** src/planner/filter/expression_filter.cpp, src/storage/table/column_segment.cpp

**Expected gain.** q19 -7..-11% (prior run); small gains for other OR-range filters

**Generality.** Any scan with an OR of range/equality predicates on one column (IN-ranges, bucketed filters). It could be slightly slower on tiny vectors with very sparse selections, which the gather handles.

**Risk.** Low-medium. Contained in the filter evaluation path; correctness of NULL handling must be checked.



## How your change will be judged

4. Correctness. Results must equal the original engine's on the visible queries, on new parameter

   different size, and on a suite of generic SQL over an unrelated schema.
5. Review. An independent reviewer reads your diff looking for benchmark-specific shortcuts and
   correctness holes.
6. Timing. Interleaved runs against the current best. The bar was fixed in advance:
   at least one visible template at least 5% faster (more for templates whose timings are noisy); no template, visible or hidden, slower by more than 5% (again more for noisy ones); and the visible geomean not slower. A change that makes one kind of query clearly faster and nothing slower is accepted.

So the only changes that survive are ones that make the engine faster for a whole class of
queries. Read `.dbe/knowledge/rules.md` before you start; it is short.
